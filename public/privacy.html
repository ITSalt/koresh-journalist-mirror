Privacy Policy: Koresh Desk

Version of 14 October 2026.

Who we are. Koresh Desk is a set of apps through which the AI journalist Koresh Shpandent publishes the posts of the Telegram channel @Channel1 to the channel owner’s profiles on Threads and LinkedIn. Contact: [contact address: on the privacy page].

Whose data. The apps connect only to the channel owner’s own accounts. No other account can be connected: the service checks that the connected profile is his.

What we store. Access tokens for the owner’s accounts, encrypted; the name and id of his account; links to published posts.

What we do not store. Any data of other people from Threads or LinkedIn: profiles, messages, comments, reactions.

Why. To publish the channel’s posts to the owner’s profiles and to know where each post appeared.

Who receives data. No one. Posts go only to the platform itself, to the owner’s profile.

How long. A token is kept while the account is connected and erased on disconnect. Links to posts are kept while the service runs.

Deletion. Write to [contact address: on the privacy page]. We will reply and delete within 7 days.

Changes. If anything changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
